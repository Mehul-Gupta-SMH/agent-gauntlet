--- 4. the curve is a property of separation, not of noise alone --------
  slip 0.22/0.08  (as-shipped)  bar first cleared at k=4
  slip 0.11/0.04  (half      )  bar first cleared at k=20
  slip 0.44/0.16  (double    )  bar first cleared at k=3

  Doubling the slip rate does not double the k needed. It widens the
  gap between the two model levels at the same time, because a
  Bernoulli policy cannot move its variance without moving its mean.
  The honest reading: k tracks the SEPARATION between adjacent
  configurations relative to the noise, and neither term is knowable
  before the run. Which is the argument for measuring the curve on
  the task at hand rather than carrying a number over from this one.

  total offline runs executed: 177,408

--- what this does not show ---------------------------------------------
  Scripted policies, declared slip rates, one fixture. What is above
  transfers as a METHOD -- sweep k, read top-1, stop where it
  flattens, refuse the depth that never flattens -- and not as a
  value of k for a live matrix, which is still unmeasured (#33).
