"How many times should I run each config?" I swept it properly instead of guessing. 177,408 runs later, two of the three answers were the opposite of what I expected.

Eight seeds, twelve budgets, one task. The board stops moving with the seed at k=4. I had been using 3.

    k   runs   med tau   top-1  decidable  verdict
    1     48     0.627     57%    28/28    fail
    3    144     0.699    100%    15/28    fail
    4    192     0.717    100%    28/28    PASS
   50   2400     0.775    100%    28/28    PASS

Three things fall out, and only the first was the question.

1. Rank correlation is the wrong number to watch. It crawls from 0.63 to 0.78 across a 50x budget increase and crosses its bar somewhere you cannot point at. "Is the winner the winner" goes 57% -> 57% -> 100% and stays. One is a slow blur, the other is a decision.

2. No budget fixes a tie. Ranks 3, 4 and 5 are separated by four thousandths:

     3   0.4642
     4   0.4629
     5   0.4604

They never settle. Not at k=50, with 2,400 runs behind every cell. They are tied in truth, and a board printing them in order is reporting noise with a confident face — one row below where anyone looks. So the fix is not more runs, it is refusing to claim an order the data does not carry.

3. Quieter agents needed FIVE TIMES more repeats, not fewer.

  slip 0.22/0.08  ->  bar cleared at k=4
  slip 0.11/0.04  ->  bar cleared at k=20
  slip 0.44/0.16  ->  bar cleared at k=3

Halving the noise also halved the gap between the configs being compared, because a Bernoulli process cannot move its variance without moving its mean. Which is the real finding: k tracks the separation between adjacent configs relative to their noise, and neither term is knowable before you run.

A k carried over from someone else's benchmark is worth nothing. Sweep it on yours.

The awkward part: this is measured against scripted policies at a declared noise level. It is a property of the measuring machinery, not a claim about real agents. What transfers is the method, and I would rather say that than let the number travel further than the evidence.
